Seal: an attester on the Ethereum Attestation Service whose attestations are made, and revoked, by GenLayer consensus. Anybody calls seal(recipient, claim, source); every validator renders the page and they must agree, compared in code. If the page states the claim, Seal writes the attestation to the official EAS contracts itself, through its gate on GenLayer Chain, with no relayer or attester key. Anybody can call recheck later: if the page no longer states it, or is gone, Seal revokes its own attestation. A failed read changes nothing, refusing needs the whole page, and a revoked record cannot be laundered back. Proven on Bradbury around a real change: a certificate register was attested, then revoked at the source, and Seal revoked the EAS attestation itself; a page ordering validators to attest was refused; all transactions are final, with source matching GitHub and the official EAS package.
